Now that the motors were done, it was time to answer a lot of new design questions. But one pressing issue on my mind is that I have never actually flown FPV before. I decided to get Liftoff, a simulator on Steam, in order to do some practice flying before I finished the real deal. I'm actually really excited to start. The only thing is that apparently the Taranis QX7 does not come with batteries and actually requires a very specific battery I do not have. I never noticed since I did not get to this part of the project last time. I got [this battery](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07L87FMX6?ref=ppx_yo2ov_dt_b_fed_asin_title) and the DJI Goggles 3 off of eBay to go with my incoming O4 Air Unit. Combining all three, it was quite the purchase day, exceeding $500, however most of that is the goggles. Due to the nightmare that is obtaining DJI now, the goggles arrive with the O4 in a couple of weeks. The battery arrived much sooner, and I was soon able to power on my QX7 and begin practicing.
